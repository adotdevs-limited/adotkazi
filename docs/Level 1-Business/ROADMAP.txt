# AdotKazi Product Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap defines the strategic evolution of AdotKazi from an internal recruitment platform for Adotdevs Limited into a production-grade, AI-powered, multi-tenant SaaS platform serving organizations across East Africa and beyond.

The roadmap provides direction for product planning, engineering priorities, design decisions, and future expansion.

It is a living document and may evolve as customer needs, market conditions, and business priorities change.

---

# Engineering Priorities

The engineering team should prioritize work in the following order:

1. Platform stability
2. Security
3. Correctness
4. Accessibility
5. Performance
6. Developer experience
7. User experience
8. New features

New functionality must never compromise platform quality.

---

# Product Principles

Every roadmap decision should align with these principles:

* Multi-tenant by design.
* AI assists but never replaces human decision-making.
* Candidate experience matters as much as recruiter experience.
* Configuration is preferred over customization.
* Accessibility is mandatory.
* Cloud provider independence is maintained.
* APIs remain reusable.
* Security is built in from the start.
* Performance is considered during design, not after implementation.

---

# Long-Term Vision

AdotKazi aims to become the leading AI-powered recruitment platform in East Africa before expanding into global markets.

The platform will remain focused on talent acquisition rather than evolving into a full Human Resource Management System (HRMS).

By maintaining a focused product vision, scalable architecture, and exceptional user experience, AdotKazi will enable organizations of every size to recruit, evaluate, and onboard talent through one intelligent, collaborative, and secure platform.
