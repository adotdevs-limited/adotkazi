# AdotKazi Opportunity Engine

## Purpose

The Opportunity Engine is the core domain responsible for creating, managing, publishing, and lifecycle management of all opportunities on the AdotKazi platform.

Rather than treating jobs as the primary entity, AdotKazi treats every opportunity as a first-class domain object.

This enables the platform to support recruitment, industrial training, internships, volunteer programs, graduate programs, and future opportunity types using a single, extensible architecture.

The Opportunity Engine is shared by multiple business domains.

---

# Vision

One engine.

Many opportunity types.

Instead of building separate systems for jobs, internships, and IPT, the platform provides one unified opportunity platform with configurable behavior.

---

# Goals

The Opportunity Engine must:

* Support unlimited opportunity types
* Be organization-aware
* Be workflow-driven
* Be AI-ready
* Be highly configurable
* Scale to millions of opportunities
* Publish domain events
* Integrate with Search, Analytics, Notifications, AI, and Workflow Engine

---

# Security

The Opportunity Engine enforces:

* Tenant isolation
* Organization ownership
* Role-based authorization
* Audit logging
* Soft deletion

---

# Performance

The engine should:

* Support millions of opportunities.
* Cache frequently accessed metadata.
* Publish events asynchronously.
* Optimize read-heavy queries.
* Use cursor pagination.
* Index searchable attributes.

# Definition of Done

The Opportunity Engine is considered production-ready when:

* All opportunity types share a unified domain model.
* Organizations can configure supported opportunity types.
* Opportunity templates are reusable.
* Workflows are configurable.
* Eligibility rules are enforced.
* Application forms are configurable.
* AI, Search, Analytics, Notifications, and Workflow Engine are integrated.
* Domain events are published for significant state changes.
* Tenant isolation is enforced.
* The engine remains extensible without requiring architectural changes for new opportunity types.
