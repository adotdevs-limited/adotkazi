# AdotKazi Industrial Practical Training (IPT) Module

## Purpose

The IPT Module manages the complete lifecycle of Industrial Practical Training (IPT), Field Training, Attachments, Internships, and other structured learning placements.

This module is one of the platform's major differentiators.

Unlike traditional ATS systems that stop after placement, the IPT Module manages the entire training journey from application through completion and evaluation.

The module is designed for:

* Students
* Training Institutions
* Organizations
* Supervisors
* Coordinators

---

# Vision

Create the most complete digital Industrial Practical Training platform in Africa.

The platform should manage:

* Opportunity Discovery
* Applications
* Placement
* Supervision
* Daily Activities
* Weekly Reports
* Final Reports
* Evaluations
* Completion
* Certification

within one unified system.

Opportunity Types:

* Industrial Practical Training
* Field Training

# Application Process

Students may apply to IPT opportunities.

Required fields may include:

* Institution
* Program
* Level of Study
* Year of Study
* Resume
* Cover Letter
* Academic Transcript
* Recommendation Letter

Organizations may customize requirements.

# Placement

Placement begins when an application is accepted.

A Placement contains:

* Student
* Organization
* Opportunity
* Department
* Branch
* Supervisor
* Start Date
* End Date
* Status

A Placement is the central aggregate of the IPT Module.

---

# Placement Status

Supported states:

* Pending
* Approved
* Active
* Suspended
* Completed
* Cancelled

---

# Supervisor Assignment

Organizations assign supervisors.

Supervisor responsibilities:

* Guide students
* Review work
* Approve reports
* Submit evaluations
* Track attendance

A supervisor may manage multiple students.

Capacity limits may be configured.

# Daily Activity Logs

Students submit daily activities.

* Date
* Activity Description
* Skills Learned
* Hours Worked
* Attachments
* Notes

# Weekly Reports

Students submit weekly reports.

Report contents:

* Activities Performed
* Skills Acquired
* Challenges Encountered
* Solutions Implemented
* Lessons Learned
* Supervisor Comments

Organizations may customize templates.

---

# AI-Assisted Weekly Reports

Students may use AI assistance.

Capabilities:

* Grammar correction
* Formatting
* Structure suggestions
* Reflection enhancement
* Professional writing assistance

AI never submits reports automatically.

Students remain responsible for final submissions.

---

# Final Report

The final report summarizes the entire placement.

Sections may include:

* Introduction
* Organization Overview
* Objectives
* Activities
* Skills Acquired
* Challenges
* Recommendations
* Conclusion

Templates may vary by institution.

---

# AI Report Assistant

The AI Platform can assist with:

* Report drafting
* Formatting
* Grammar
* Executive summaries
* Skill extraction
* Learning outcome analysis

All generated content requires student review.

---

# Attendance Tracking

# Definition of Done

The IPT Module is considered production-ready when:

* Students can apply for IPT opportunities.
* Placements are managed through a structured lifecycle.
* Supervisors can monitor and evaluate students.
* Daily logs and weekly reports are supported.
* Final reports are managed digitally.
* AI assists report creation and evaluation.
* Certificates can be generated.
* Notifications, Search, Analytics, and Workflow Engine are integrated.
* Domain events are published.
* Tenant isolation is enforced.
* The architecture supports future institutional integrations.
