# INTERVIEWS.md

# AdotKazi Interview Management Domain

## Purpose

The Interview Management Domain manages the complete interview lifecycle for all opportunity types on the AdotKazi platform.

It provides a flexible, configurable interview system that supports recruitment, internships, graduate programs, apprenticeships, and future opportunity types.

The Interview domain owns interview scheduling, interviewer assignments, feedback collection, scorecards, evaluations, and interview decisions.

---

# Vision

Create a modern, collaborative interview platform that enables organizations to conduct structured, fair, and data-driven interviews while integrating seamlessly with Recruitment, AI, Notifications, Analytics, and the Workflow Engine.

---

# Goals

The Interview domain must:

* Support unlimited interview stages
* Support multiple interview types
* Support panel interviews
* Support structured scorecards
* Support interviewer collaboration
* Integrate with calendars
* Integrate with AI
* Scale to millions of interviews
* Be fully tenant-aware

---

# Core Philosophy

Interviews are independent domain objects.

Applications move through interview stages, but interviews maintain their own lifecycle, scheduling, participants, feedback, recordings, and outcomes.

---

# Interview Lifecycle

```text
Application Qualified

↓

Interview Planned

↓

Interview Scheduled

↓

Invitations Sent

↓

Interview Confirmed

↓

Interview Conducted

↓

Feedback Submitted

↓

Decision Recorded

↓

Next Stage or Completed
```

* Domain events are published for all significant actions.
* Tenant isolation is enforced.
* The architecture supports future real-time interview capabilities.
