# AdotKazi Content Management System (CMS)

## Purpose

The Content Management System (CMS) enables organizations and platform administrators to create, manage, publish, and localize content across the AdotKazi platform without requiring code changes.

Unlike a traditional website CMS, the AdotKazi CMS is a **Headless, Multi-Tenant, API-First Content Platform** that powers careers websites, landing pages, email content, help documentation, legal pages, and other dynamic content.

The CMS serves both the AdotKazi platform and each organization's public careers experience.

---

# Vision

Provide every organization with the ability to build and manage a professional careers presence while giving the platform a flexible foundation for future websites, portals, and marketing pages.

The CMS should be extensible enough to support future products without redesign.

---

# Goals

The CMS must:

* Be multi-tenant
* Be headless
* Be API-first
* Support localization
* Support versioning
* Support workflows
* Support media management
* Support SEO
* Support reusable content
* Support future page builders

---

# Core Principles

The CMS owns content.

Business domains own business data.

Example:

Recruitment owns:

* Opportunities
* Applications
* Interviews

CMS owns:

* Homepage
* About Company
* Benefits
* FAQs
* Team Descriptions
* Culture Content

Business logic must never live inside the CMS.

# Definition of Done

The CMS is considered production-ready when:

* Organizations can manage careers website content.
* Content is delivered through APIs.
* Versioning and publishing workflows are implemented.
* Localization is supported.
* SEO metadata is configurable.
* Media is managed through the Document Service.
* AI, Search, Analytics, Notifications, and Workflow Engine are integrated.
* Tenant isolation is enforced.
* Audit logging is enabled.
* The architecture supports future page builders and additional content types without structural redesign.
