# ORGANIZATIONS.md

# AdotKazi Organization Domain

## Purpose

The Organization Domain is the foundational domain of the AdotKazi platform.

Every tenant represents an organization, and every business operation occurs within the context of an organization.

The Organization Domain manages organization identity, structure, branding, settings, memberships, branches, departments, policies, quotas, and configuration.

It acts as the root boundary for multi-tenancy.

---

# Vision

Enable any organization—from a small startup to a multinational enterprise—to configure AdotKazi as its own recruitment and talent platform without affecting any other organization.

Organizations should feel like they have their own dedicated recruitment system while sharing the same platform.

---

# Goals

The Organization Domain must:

* Support unlimited organizations
* Provide complete tenant isolation
* Support multiple branches
* Support multiple departments
* Support organization branding
* Support organization settings
* Support memberships and permissions
* Support future subscriptions
* Scale independently

---

# Core Philosophy

Everything belongs to an organization.

Examples:

* Opportunities
* Applications
* Candidates
* Recruiters
* Documents
* Interviews
* Notifications
* Analytics
* AI Usage
* Settings
* Workflows

Every entity references:

```text
organization_id
```

The organization is the root ownership boundary.

---

# Domain Responsibilities

The Organization Domain owns:

* Organization Profile
* Organization Settings
* Branding
* Membership
* Branches
* Departments
* Locations
* Working Hours
* Hiring Preferences
* Email Configuration
* Feature Configuration
* Subscription Metadata
* Custom Domains

The Organization Domain does **not** own:

* Opportunities
* Applications
* Candidates
* Interviews
* Reports

Those belong to their respective domains.

# Definition of Done

The Organization Domain is considered production-ready when:

* Every resource belongs to an organization.
* Organizations can manage branding, departments, branches, and settings.
* Global user accounts support membership in multiple organizations.
* Feature flags and opportunity types are configurable.
* Custom domains are supported.
* AI, Analytics, Search, Notifications, and Workflow Engine are integrated.
* Tenant isolation is enforced across all operations.
* Organization events are published for significant lifecycle changes.
* The domain remains independent of billing and infrastructure providers, enabling future SaaS growth.
