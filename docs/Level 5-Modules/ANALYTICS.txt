# ANALYTICS.md

# AdotKazi Analytics & Business Intelligence Platform

## Purpose

The Analytics Platform is a cross-platform service responsible for collecting, processing, analyzing, and visualizing operational and business data across the AdotKazi ecosystem.

It provides real-time and historical insights for organizations, recruiters, supervisors, and platform administrators.

Analytics is a read-only domain that consumes events from other domains. It never owns business data.

---

# Vision

Provide every organization with actionable insights that improve hiring, training, productivity, and operational efficiency.

Analytics should answer questions such as:

* Where are candidates dropping off?
* Which recruitment sources perform best?
* How long does hiring take?
* Which IPT supervisors have the best outcomes?
* Which departments hire the fastest?
* What skills are most in demand?
* Which AI features provide the most value?

---

# Goals

The Analytics Platform must:

* Support real-time dashboards
* Generate historical reports
* Track KPIs
* Support custom dashboards
* Be organization-aware
* Scale to billions of events
* Support AI-generated insights
* Export reports
* Support scheduled reporting

# Architecture

```text
                    Domain Events

 Recruitment   IPT   AI   Communication

 Search   Opportunity   Interviews

          Workflow   Platform

                │

                ▼

          Event Processing

                │

                ▼

         Analytics Pipeline

                │

     ┌──────────┼──────────┐

     │          │          │

 Data Warehouse KPI Engine Report Engine

                │

                ▼

         Dashboards & APIs
```

Analytics consumes events.

Business domains never write directly into analytics tables.

# Data Retention

Analytics retention policies are configurable.

Historical aggregates may be retained longer than operational events.

Raw event retention is configurable based on organization policy.

---

# Performance

The Analytics Platform should:

* Use pre-aggregated metrics where possible.
* Support asynchronous report generation.
* Cache frequently accessed dashboards.
* Separate transactional and analytical workloads.
* Scale independently of business services.

# Definition of Done

The Analytics Platform is considered production-ready when:

* Analytics is implemented as a separate read-only domain.
* Data is collected through domain events.
* Dashboards support real-time and historical reporting.
* Recruitment, IPT, Interviews, AI, Communication, and Platform metrics are available.
* KPI calculations are standardized.
* Reports can be exported and scheduled.
* AI-generated insights are supported.
* Tenant isolation is enforced.
* Performance is optimized for analytical workloads.
* The platform is extensible for future predictive analytics and enterprise BI integrations.
