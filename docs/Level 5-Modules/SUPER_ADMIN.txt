# AdotKazi Super Administration Domain

## Purpose

The Super Administration Domain is responsible for managing the AdotKazi platform itself.

Unlike Organization Administration, which manages a single tenant, the Super Administration Domain manages the entire SaaS platform, including organizations, platform users, subscriptions, infrastructure settings, feature rollouts, system health, AI providers, and global governance.

The Super Admin never directly manages an organization's day-to-day recruitment activities unless explicitly performing support or impersonation.

---

# Vision

Provide a secure, scalable, and auditable control center for operating AdotKazi as a global SaaS platform.

The Super Admin Console should function as the platform's "mission control."

---

# Goals

The Super Administration Domain must:

* Manage all organizations
* Manage platform-wide configuration
* Monitor system health
* Control feature rollouts
* Manage AI providers
* Manage integrations
* Support customer success
* Support billing (future)
* Support platform analytics
* Be fully audited

---

# Core Principles

## Platform-Level Authority

Super Administrators operate above every organization.

They manage the platform—not the organizations' business operations.

Example:

```text id="sa1"
Platform

├── Organization A

├── Organization B

├── Organization C

└── Organization D
```

Organizations remain isolated from each other.

---

## Least Privilege

Even Super Admins should have scoped permissions.

Examples:

* Platform Owner
* Platform Administrator
* Support Engineer
* DevOps Engineer
* Finance Administrator
* Customer Success
* Security Administrator
* Read-Only Auditor

No role automatically has unrestricted access.

# Definition of Done

The Super Administration Domain is considered production-ready when:

* Platform administrators can manage organizations without violating tenant isolation.
* Platform configuration is centralized.
* Feature flags are managed globally and per organization.
* AI providers, integrations, and infrastructure settings are configurable.
* Platform analytics and health monitoring are available.
* Support impersonation is secure and fully audited.
* Notifications, Analytics, Search, AI Platform, and Workflow Engine are integrated.
* All privileged actions are logged and protected by strong security controls.
* The architecture supports future enterprise SaaS operations without major redesign.
