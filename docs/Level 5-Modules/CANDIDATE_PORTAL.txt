# AdotKazi Candidate Portal

## Purpose

The Candidate Portal is the primary interface for individuals using the AdotKazi platform to discover opportunities, manage their professional profile, submit applications, track progress, communicate with organizations, and build a long-term career portfolio.

Unlike traditional ATS portals that are application-centric, the AdotKazi Candidate Portal is **candidate-centric**. A candidate creates one global profile that can be used across multiple organizations and opportunities.

The portal is shared across the platform and is not owned by any single organization.

---

# Vision

Provide every candidate with a single, intelligent career hub where they can:

* Discover opportunities
* Apply once using reusable profiles
* Track applications
* Build their professional portfolio
* Complete IPT placements
* Receive AI career assistance
* Manage documents
* Grow throughout their career

---

# Goals

The Candidate Portal must:

* Support one global candidate account
* Provide reusable candidate profiles
* Support multiple applications
* Support IPT and Recruitment
* Be mobile-friendly
* Integrate with AI
* Support notifications
* Scale to millions of users

# Core Principles

## One Global Account

Each candidate owns one global account.

Example:

```text id="cp1"
John Doe

↓

Global Candidate Account

↓

Applications

├── Adotdevs

├── Company A

├── Company B

├── Government Agency

└── NGO
```

Candidates never create separate accounts for each organization.

---

## Profile Once, Apply Everywhere

Candidates maintain one professional profile.

Organizations read the profile but cannot permanently modify it.

Profile changes automatically apply to future applications.

# Definition of Done

The Candidate Portal is considered production-ready when:

* Candidates have one global account and reusable profile.
* Applications can be managed across multiple organizations.
* Recruitment and IPT workflows are fully supported.
* Documents are managed through the Document Service.
* AI provides career assistance and opportunity recommendations.
* Search, Notifications, Analytics, Workflow Engine, and Recommendation Engine are integrated.
* Privacy controls are configurable.
* Tenant isolation and security are enforced.
* The portal is responsive, accessible, and designed to scale to millions of candidates.
