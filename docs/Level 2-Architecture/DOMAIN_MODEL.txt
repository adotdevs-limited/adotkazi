# DOMAIN_MODEL.md

# AdotKazi Domain Model

## Purpose

This document defines the business language of AdotKazi.

It describes the core business concepts, ownership rules, relationships, lifecycle rules, and domain boundaries that govern the platform.

This document is the source of truth for:

* Database design
* APIs
* Services
* Business rules
* Authorization
* Events
* Future features

Implementation details must never contradict the domain model.

---

# Domain Philosophy

AdotKazi is a Talent Acquisition Platform.

The platform manages opportunities, applications, recruitment workflows, interviews, and internship placements through one unified domain model.

Every feature should extend the domain rather than introducing isolated business concepts.

---

# Bounded Contexts

The platform is divided into bounded contexts.

## Platform

Responsible for:

* Global users
* Organizations
* Memberships
* Authentication
* Billing (future)
* Platform administration

---

## Recruitment

Responsible for:

* Opportunities
* Applications
* Candidates
* Recruiters
* Pipelines
* Interviews
* Hiring

---

## Internship (IPT)

Responsible for:

* Student placements
* University information
* Supervisors

* Evaluations
* Reports
* Certificates

---

## AI

Responsible for:

* Resume parsing
* Candidate summaries
* Matching

## Communication

## Administration

# Core Aggregates

## Organization

Represents a company using AdotKazi.

Owns:

* Branches
* Departments
* Recruiters
* Opportunities
* Pipelines
* Branding
* Settings
* Analytics

Organization is the primary aggregate root for tenant-owned data.

---

## Branch

Represents a physical or operational location belonging to an organization.

Examples:

* Headquarters
* Regional Office
* Branch Office

Opportunities may belong to a branch.

Branches inherit organization ownership.

---

## Department

Represents an organizational department.

Examples:

* Engineering
* Marketing
* Finance
* Human Resources

Departments are organization-wide and may be assigned to opportunities.

---

## Membership

Represents a user's relationship with an organization.

Contains:

* Role
* Permissions
* Status
* Invitation state

A user may have multiple memberships across different organizations.

---

## Opportunity

Represents any recruitment opportunity.

Supported types include:

* Full-Time
* Part-Time
* Contract
* Internship (IPT)
* Graduate Program
* Apprenticeship
* Volunteer

Every opportunity belongs to:

* Organization
* Department
* Optional Branch

Every opportunity has:

* Hiring pipeline
* Recruiters
* Applications
* Status
* Visibility

Opportunity is a primary aggregate root.

---

## Candidate

Represents a person seeking opportunities.

Candidates own:

* Personal information
* Skills
* Resume
* Portfolio
* Experience
* Education
* Certifications

Candidates maintain one reusable global profile.

Candidates may apply to multiple organizations and multiple opportunities.

---

## Application

Represents a candidate applying for an opportunity.

Application belongs to:

* Candidate
* Opportunity

Application owns:

* Current pipeline stage
* Status
* Notes
* Attachments
* Interview history
* Timeline

Applications are immutable records of recruitment activity.

---

## Pipeline

Represents the hiring workflow.

Organizations own reusable pipeline templates.

Every opportunity references one pipeline.

Opportunities may customize pipeline stages without affecting the template.

---

## Interview

Represents an interview event.

Belongs to:

* Application

Contains:

* Interviewers
* Schedule
* Feedback
* Scores
* Outcome

Multiple interviews may exist for one application.

---

## Placement (IPT)

Represents an accepted internship placement.

Contains:

* Student
* Supervisor
* Organization
* Branch
* Department
* Start date
* End date
* Objectives

Placement begins only after a successful internship application.

---

## Supervisor

Represents an employee responsible for supervising interns.

Supervisors may oversee multiple placements.

Supervisors complete:

* Evaluations
* Approvals
* Feedback

---

## Evaluation

Represents structured assessment.

Supports:

* Internship evaluation
* Interview evaluation
* Candidate scoring

Evaluations remain immutable after final submission.

---

# Supporting Aggregates

## Document

Represents any uploaded or generated document.

Supported types include:

* Resume
* Cover Letter
* Certificate
* Transcript
* Introduction Letter
* Contract
* Offer Letter
* Generated Report

Documents have:

* Owner
* Type
* Storage location
* Metadata
* Permissions
* Version history

Business entities reference documents rather than storing file information directly.

---

## Notification

Represents communication delivered to users.

Version 1 supports email.

Future providers:

* WhatsApp
* SMS
* Push

Notifications remain provider-independent.

---

## Email Template

Represents reusable communication templates.

Organizations own their templates.

Templates support variables and localization.

---

## AI Task

Represents AI processing requests.

Examples:

* Resume parsing
* Candidate summary
* Report generation

AI Tasks store requests and results but never modify business entities directly without user confirmation.

---

## Audit Event

Represents immutable historical records.

Examples:

* Opportunity published
* Candidate applied
* Interview scheduled
* Offer accepted

Audit events cannot be modified.

---

## Integration

Represents external services.

Examples:

* Google Workspace
* Google Meet
* Zoom
* WhatsApp

Integrations remain isolated from core business logic.

---

---

# Business Invariants

The following rules must always hold true:

* Every tenant-owned record belongs to exactly one organization.
* Every application belongs to exactly one candidate and one opportunity.
* Every opportunity belongs to exactly one organization.
* A candidate owns only one global profile.
* Organizations cannot modify candidate-owned profile data.
* AI cannot make hiring decisions.
* Deleted records must preserve audit history.
* Business entities never communicate directly with external providers.
* Every critical action creates an audit event.

---

# Future Evolution

The domain model is designed for long-term growth.

Future features—including recruitment agencies, university partnerships, public APIs, automation, billing, and marketplace integrations—must extend the existing domain model rather than replacing it.

Changes to the domain should prioritize backward compatibility, maintainability, and consistency across the platform.
