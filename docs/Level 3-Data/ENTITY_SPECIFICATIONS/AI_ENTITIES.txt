# AI_ENTITIES.md

# Artificial Intelligence Entity Specifications

## Purpose

The AI bounded context provides intelligent capabilities across the AdotKazi platform while ensuring that AI remains an assistant rather than a decision-maker.

AI services are shared across all bounded contexts, including:

* Recruitment
* Internship (IPT)
* Communication
* Analytics
* CMS
* Platform

The AI domain owns AI requests, processing, outputs, feedback, models, prompts, and recommendations.

It never owns business entities.

---

# AI Philosophy

Artificial Intelligence must:

* Assist users.
* Increase productivity.
* Reduce repetitive work.
* Improve decision quality.

Artificial Intelligence must never:

* Hire candidates.
* Reject candidates.
* Approve placements.
* Modify business records automatically.
* Replace human judgement.

Every AI-generated output must be reviewable and editable before acceptance.

# Shared AI Standards

All AI entities must:

* Support tenant isolation.
* Support audit logging.
* Never expose prompts between organizations.
* Respect feature flags.
* Allow provider replacement.
* Store model version.
* Track processing cost.
* Support asynchronous execution.

---

# Performance Considerations

* Queue long-running requests.
* Cache repeated prompt templates.
* Stream large responses where supported.
* Record latency metrics.
* Monitor token usage.
* Archive historical AI responses according to retention policies.

---

# Security

Prompt injection protection must be implemented.

Sensitive organizational data must never leak between tenants.

AI providers must receive only the minimum information necessary for the requested task.

Personally identifiable information should be masked or minimized whenever practical.
